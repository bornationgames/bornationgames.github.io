Delete Your Data

Real Shot: Not Fake, by Bornation · Last updated: October 15, 2026

This page explains how to delete the data related to the mobile game Real Shot: Not Fake (the "Game"), published by Bornation. The Game has no account and no login, so there is no account to delete.

How to request deletion

  1. Send an email to bornation.support@gmail.com with the subject "Real Shot data deletion".
  2. Say which device you play on (Android or iPhone). We don't need your name or any other personal details.
  3. We answer within 30 days. We delete your request email once it has been handled.

Delete it yourself, right now

  • Game data (XP, upgrades, settings, your advertising choice): it is stored only on your device. Uninstall the Game, or clear its data in your phone's settings, and it is deleted at once.
  • Advertising data: reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings (Android: Settings → Google → Ads; iPhone: Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking), and turn off Personalized ads in the Game's Settings.

What is deleted, and what is kept

DataWhat happens
Game progress and settingsStored only on your device. Deleted when you uninstall the Game or clear its data. We never have a copy.
Advertising data collected by Unity (advertising ID, approximate location, device information, ad interactions) On request, we ask Unity to delete the data linked to your device. Unity keeps it no longer than described in the Unity privacy policy. You can also contact Unity directly through that page.
Your "Remove Ads" purchaseKept by Google Play or the Apple App Store with your store account, so the purchase can be restored and for their legal and tax obligations. We don't hold any payment data.
Your deletion request emailDeleted once your request has been handled.
