Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 3, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the mobile game Real Shot (the "Game") handles your information. The Game is published by Bornation ("we", "us"), based in Spain. If you have any question, contact us at bornation.support@gmail.com.

1. The short version

  • We don't ask for your name, email, or any account. The Game has no login.
  • Your progress (XP, upgrades, settings) is saved only on your device. We never receive it.
  • The Game is free and shows ads. Our advertising partner (Unity) collects some device data to show and measure ads. When you first open the Game, you choose whether ads can be personalized, and you can change this anytime in Settings.
  • You can buy "Remove Ads" in the Game. Payment is handled by Google Play or the Apple App Store; we never see your payment details.
  • We don't sell your personal information.

2. Information stored on your device

The Game saves your game progress (XP, upgrades, tutorial status), your advertising choice and whether you bought Remove Ads on your device. This data never leaves your device and is not sent to us. You can delete it at any time by uninstalling the Game or clearing its data in your phone's settings.

3. Advertising

The Game shows ads (rewarded ads you choose to watch, and ads between games) through Unity LevelPlay, an ad mediation service, and the ad networks Unity Ads and ironSource Ads. All three are operated by Unity Technologies and its affiliates ("Unity").

To deliver ads, Unity may collect the following information directly from your device:

  • your device's advertising identifier (Google Advertising ID on Android; on iPhone, Apple's IDFA only if you allow tracking when iOS asks) and other device identifiers;
  • your IP address and approximate location (country) derived from it;
  • device information: model, manufacturer, operating system version, language, time zone, mobile carrier, connection type, free storage and battery level;
  • information about the ads you see and how you interact with them (views, clicks, completions).

This information is used to show ads, limit how often you see the same ad, prevent fraud, measure ad performance, and pay us for the ads shown. If you consent, it can also be used to show ads that match your interests (personalized ads).

We don't receive this data in a way that identifies you. Unity processes it under its own privacy policy: Unity Game Player and App User Privacy Policy.

4. In-app purchase (Remove Ads)

You can buy Remove Ads in the Game (Settings), which stops the ads shown between games. Rewarded ads, which you choose to watch for a bonus, stay available.

Payment is handled entirely by Google Play (Android) or the Apple App Store (iPhone). We never see your name, email or payment details. The store tells the Game only which product you own, with a purchase receipt. Your payment is processed under the Google Privacy Policy or Apple's Privacy Policy.

The Game uses Unity's in-app purchasing library (Unity IAP) to talk to the store. This library may send technical information to Unity (such as device model, operating system, the product bought and whether the purchase succeeded) to operate and improve the service, as described in Unity's privacy policy.

Your purchase is saved on your device and linked to your Google account or Apple Account by the store, so it comes back if you reinstall the Game. You can also tap Restore purchases in Settings. For refunds, see Google Play's or Apple's refund policy, or contact us.

5. Your choice about personalized ads

The first time you open the Game, we ask whether you accept personalized ads. The Game shows ads either way:

  • Accept: ads can be based on your interests.
  • No thanks: ads are not personalized. We also ask our partners not to sell or share your personal information.

On iPhone, iOS then asks whether the Game may track you (App Tracking Transparency). If you don't allow it, the advertising identifier (IDFA) is not shared with our ad partners.

You can change your choice anytime in the Game: Settings → Personalized ads (ON / OFF). You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings (on most Android phones: Settings → Google → Ads; on iPhone: Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking).

7. Sharing and international transfers

We don't share your information with anyone other than Unity (ads and in-app purchase library) and Google Play or Apple (payments), as described above. Unity may share ad-related data with advertisers and measurement partners, and may transfer it outside your country (including to the United States) with appropriate safeguards, as explained in Unity's privacy policy.

8. Data retention

Data saved on your device stays there until you uninstall the Game or clear its data. We keep no copy. Advertising data is kept by Unity according to its privacy policy.

9. Children

The Game is not directed at children under 13 (or under 14 in Spain). We don't knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has used the Game in a way that provided personal information, contact us and we'll help remove it.

10. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR in Europe or the CCPA in California), you may have the right to access, correct, delete or port your personal information, to object to or restrict its processing, to withdraw your consent, and to opt out of the "sale" or "sharing" of personal information.

  • For data saved in the Game: uninstall the Game or clear its data.
  • For advertising data: use the in-game setting above, reset your advertising ID, or contact Unity as described in its privacy policy.
  • For any other request, email us at bornation.support@gmail.com. We'll answer within one month.

If you are in Spain or elsewhere in the EU, you can also file a complaint with a data protection authority, such as the Spanish Agencia Española de Protección de Datos (www.aepd.es).

11. Security

We don't run servers that store your information. Your progress stays on your device, protected by your phone's security. Unity protects the advertising data it processes as described in its privacy policy.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy, for example if we add new features. The date at the top shows the latest version. Important changes will also be announced in the Game or on its store page.
